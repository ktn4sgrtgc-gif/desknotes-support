Where are my notes stored?

Notes are stored locally in your Mac’s Application Support folder. If iCloud sync is enabled, Desk Notes stores a workspace backup in your private iCloud Drive container.

How do I restore a deleted note?

Open Recently Deleted from the note list. You can restore a note, delete it permanently, or empty the trash.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Desk Notes Pro and choose Restore purchases. Both plans are available through the same Apple ID used for the original subscription.

Why are reminders not appearing?

Allow notifications for Desk Notes in System Settings. Reminders and timed notifications are Pro features.
