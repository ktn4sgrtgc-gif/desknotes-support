Privacy Policy

Your notes belong to you.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Data stored by the app

Desk Notes stores note text, note settings, reminder choices, window positions, version history, and Recently Deleted items on your Mac. Images you choose as note backgrounds are processed locally and stored in the app’s local container.

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Desk Notes writes a workspace backup containing notes, history, Recently Deleted items, and custom backgrounds to your private iCloud Drive container. The developer does not operate a separate account or content server and does not receive this workspace data.

Purchases

Desk Notes Pro subscriptions and purchase restoration are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app does not receive or store payment card details.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Desk Notes asks macOS for notification permission and schedules local notifications. Notification content is not sent to the developer.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Desk Notes does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your notes remain on your Mac until you delete them or remove the app’s data. Permanently deleted notes and their unused custom backgrounds are removed from local storage. Items in Recently Deleted remain until you restore them or empty the trash.

External links

The support and privacy pages may link to GitHub for issue reporting and to Apple for subscription management. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Desk Notes support page.
